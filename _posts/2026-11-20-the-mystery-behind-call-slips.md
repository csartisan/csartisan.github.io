Call slips: the small but mighty messengers of school administration. Tens of little pieces of paper revolve around our campus every day to summon people to our school. Those who have received slips from the office are well aware of their colors: sometimes pink, blue, green, white, or more. But what do these colors mean, if they hold any significance at all? 

“I was in Mrs Read’s office, and I asked her if the colors of the slips meant anything,” says Musical Theater junior Paloma McGavin, who also wanted to uncover its meaning. “They [admin] put colored paper in the printer so that it’ll make people think it’s more urgent. They found that they get more responses when the call slips are colored, as opposed to when they’re white.” 

An official quote from the admin has not been clarified yet. 

Despite the function that the colored papers serve for call slips, students should still regard every slip with equal importance for good etiquette. Every and any call is worth picking up and responding to.
