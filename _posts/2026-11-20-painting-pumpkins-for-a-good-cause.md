This year’s pumpkin painting event featured some of CSArts' most talented visual arts students as they united to create and paint highly detailed and colorful pumpkins. This event was held as a silent auction to bring the community together and raise money for students and supplies for the visual arts conservatory. “The auction provides support for our conservatory, helping us continue to nurture creativity, enrich our students’ experiences, and bring our community together through a shared love of the arts,” said  Visual Arts Conservatory director Tiffany Ma. 

Not only did students enjoy making art, but they also enjoyed the memories the artwork brought back. “A lot of them made me reminisce about my early childhood. One specific pumpkin that was based on the head of Coraline,”  said sophomore Zealand Hazelate 

The event brings CSArts artists and the general student body together every year and allows everyone to show their creativity and shine brightly.
