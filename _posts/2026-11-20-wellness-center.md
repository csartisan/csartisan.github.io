The Wellness Center is a humble abode for students who are having a difficult day and a stable shelter for those who need a pick-me-up. With the stress of high school and the harshness of growing up as a teenager, it can become overwhelming and impossible to catch a break. And it can feel rougher catching a break when the break keeps slipping from you, due to the commotion just outside the doors

The Wellness Center is located in room 111.  In addition to being a place where students can quietly de-stress, school staff are there to support you.  For example, school psychologists and education specialists are present to help students through difficult times.

This school year, the tranquility of the Wellness Center has increasingly been challenged by the commotion taking place just outside its doors. Since some students visit the Wellness Center when they feel overwhelmed, anxious, or having a difficult day, they need to have a safe, quiet environment. But this can easily be disrupted when students are being loud outside the room.

Students should remember when being outside that, even though we may be on breaks, it is important that we don’t always know what someone is going through when they are in the Wellness Center.

“I think one of the biggest things to remember is that we don't always know what someone is going through when they walk through our doors. Some students might be dealing with something really difficult, while others just need a little time to themselves.” Said Education Specialist who works at the Wellness Center, Alisha Miranda.

So, to help students feel more comfortable in the Wellness Center and feel better from their day, it would be helpful to maintain a respectful volume of voice when outside the Wellness Center.

“I get really overwhelmed by loud noises sometimes. When that happens, I try the wellness center sometimes– because that’s kinda what it’s supposed to do for students. It doesn’t help as much as it’s supposed to when students outside are being loud, and it can even make me feel more overwhelmed.” Said student Jessica Pang.

While the Wellness Center is a place for students to feel more comfortable, loud conversations from students being disruptive or students hanging out outside or near the room can make it harder for students to feel better and get the support they need. Even if it may not seem like a big deal to some, it would be helpful for students to be mindful of their volume when being near the 100s or outside the Wellness Center.

The Wellness Center is meant to be a place where students can escape the noise of their everyday lives, and maintaining that environment starts with the consideration of those just outside its doors.
