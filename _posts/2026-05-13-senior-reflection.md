I would not say I always enjoyed high school. And not because of the classes, or the teacher, or the people, but because I always found myself feeling a bit stuck in a loop. Did school and home without much variation and never gave much attention to the activities offered. I now feel it was based on a deep seated insecurity I had about my talents in comparison with others. You will find yourself seeing a classmate's film, painting, or writing, or acting, or ect and feeling inferior but what I learned to do is not focus on something else, but turning that insecurity into love. I learned to love my classmates' works on all fronts and become a fan of who they are and what they did, and I find it gave me a better appreciation of the arts. I’ll never forget the opportunity I had coming to a school of creators and I hope someone else will always realize the beauty of CSArts.
