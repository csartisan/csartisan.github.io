The new cafeteria cart in the 100s might be parallel to the 200s pizza cart, but it has a range of other foods. It started on October 19 in response to long lines and a new employee. CSArtisan talked to DUSD Food Service Director Brian Volz for insight on the cart’s launch. “We wanted to offer another option for students to help reduce the wait time and increase how many students we could serve during our lunch period,” Volz said. So far, it’s been redirecting traffic effectively, so that the 200s pizza line blocks less of the hallway. 

“Campus Cuisine,” as it’s called, has a whiteboard menu displaying foods that are mostly sandwiches (if a hot dog is a sandwich, that is). The list includes hot dogs, hamburgers, enchiladas, cheese sticks, and many more options. “We are still in the process of trying different foods,” Volz said. “Any suggestions are welcome.”

Student Kat Bartholomaus gets lunch there regularly. “If you get a burger, they have banana peppers and jalapeños you can put on and condiments like ketchup and mustard,” Kat said. “The first couple of days it was mostly just a couple of things on the menu, but I feel like now they’ve figured out the plan and everything so it’s a lot nicer.” One complaint she has is that some sides, like tater tots, run out quickly.
