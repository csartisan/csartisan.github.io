Overwatch’s latest and 54th hero, Doctrine, has recently been added back to the game after a temporary removal. Many wonder why he was removed, as it was not fully clear whether he was removed to make balancing changes or if he was just simply permanently removed.

The new playable character was removed from the game on September 15th. Doctrine was revealed at Blizzard's BlizzCon event as part of the Overwatch Season 5 trailer. He came with changes to existing heroes, those being Roadhog and Sombra.

After the release of his gameplay trailer, Doctrine was temporarily put into the main game for a trial release. Although most players did not actually realize that his release was temporary as part of announcements for the upcoming Season 5, when he was removed from the game after his trial ended, many were surprised.

“...He’s difficult to play against, but I think he’s a very fun character.”, said one anonymous player who played with, as, and against Doctrine during his temporary release.

“His ultimate is really powerful, and he’s also pretty fun.”, said another player, who was also active during Doctrine’s temporary release.

After being added back in the Season 5 update on October 6th, along with the reworks for Roadhog and Sombra, a new battle pass, and a few new Halloween events, it was clear that no changes were made to his perks and abilities, which might cause discourse among the community. Players say he’s a fun addition to the game and overall a good character. Make sure to check out Doctrine, Roadhog, and Sombra next time you play.
